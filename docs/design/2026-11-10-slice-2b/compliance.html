Slice 2b mockup: Compliance by kind, and Home's orange step boxes

Invented data. The Compliance page reuses the Findings page's grid: warning kinds down, weeks across, one blue ramp, the count written in each cell. Kinds come from the reporter's structured warnings (CR4). The dashboard groups them into families, and a kind it doesn't know lands in "Other". A cell lists its warnings below the grid. A step box on Home turns orange when a warning in the diagram's period is tied to that step.
